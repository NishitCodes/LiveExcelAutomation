## 3. Core Worksheet: `SOM (05.10.2026)` (Statement of Monthly Expenditures)

* **Dimensions:** Rows 1 to ~436, Columns A to P.
* **Structure:** Comprises **26 distinct financial tables**:
  * **Table 1 (R1–R29):** Summary Sheet Revenue
  * **Table 2 (R30–R49):** Summary Sheet Capital
  * **Tables 3–26 (R50–R436):** Individual Office Breakdown Tables (Headquarters, PMIS, IPIE, RoC, OL, RD, DGCoA, IEPF, CDM, SFIO, NCLT, NCLAT for Revenue and Capital).
* **Master Date Parameter (`$P$5`):**
  * **CRITICAL:** Cell `P5` contains `05.10.2026` and cell `O5` contains `As on Date:`.
  * Exactly 511 dynamic formulas across all table titles and headers reference `$P$5` (e.g. `=CONCAT("Summary Sheet Revenue as on ", $P$5)`).
  * **NEVER overwrite, clear, or shift `$P$5`.**
* **Executive Styling Standards:**
  * **Font:** Unified on `Arial` across the entire sheet.
  * **Table Titles:** Merged across `A:K`, 12pt Bold, Dark Navy (`#1B365D`), **Center-Aligned**.
  * **Unit Labels:** Merged across `L:M`, 10pt Bold Italic, Slate Gray (`#475569`), Right-Aligned (`(Rs. In Crore)`).
  * **Super-Headers (`FY 2025-26` on C:F & `FY 2026-27` on G:K):** Deep Classic Navy (`#1B365D`) with bold white text.
  * **2-Row Vertical Merged Headers:**
    * Col A: `S.No.`
    * Col B: `Office` (Tables 1–2) / `Object Heads` (Tables 3–26)
    * Col L: `Proposed RE 26-27` (Tables 1–2) / `Demand` (Tables 3–26)
    * Col M: `Proposed BE 27-28` (Tables 1–2) / `Officer` (Tables 3–26)
  * **Data Rows:**
    * Col A (S.No.): Center-aligned, integer format `0`.
    * Col B (Offices / Heads): Left-aligned with 1-character indent (`IndentLevel = 1`).
    * Cols C–J, L (Amounts): Right-aligned, accounting format `#,##0.00;(#,##0.00);"-"`.
    * Col K (% AE): Right-aligned, percentage format `0.0%`.
    * Col M (Officer): Center-aligned text format `@`.
  * **Total Rows:** Soft accent fill (`#EBF1F8`), bold font, continuous top border, double-line accounting bottom border (`xlDouble`).
  * **Separators:** 25 clean 14pt gap rows between tables with zero stray index numbers in Column A.
